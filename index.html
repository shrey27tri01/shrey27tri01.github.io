Bio

I work on foundation models and the research and engineering involved in developing them. At ZenteiQ, I currently lead post-training work within the Dhīr foundation-model program. I work across the model-development process, with a particular focus on supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, evaluation, and the engineering needed to move experiments toward release. Our team is preparing its first 2B and 5B model releases. My broader research interests span pre-training, data, alignment, and efficient training systems. I am interested in how choices across the model-development process shape capabilities, reliability, and behavior.

My post-training work includes instruction and conversation data, tool-use behavior, synthetic-data workflows, and human-preference collection. Our team has completed initial GRPO experiments, and I am planning further preference-optimization work. I am particularly interested in connecting training signals to measurable changes in model behavior, including multilingual and India-relevant use cases.

Model safety is part of that work. I contribute to safety-data design, red-teaming, evaluation planning, and the safety section of our release documentation. This includes examining harmful responses, unnecessary refusals, jailbreak resistance, language coverage, and the limits of the evidence available for a particular checkpoint.

I also work on the systems that support research and deployment. This includes our internal data-generation and post-training platform, GPU and TPU inference with vLLM, Kubernetes-based operations, and deployment workflows. My earlier platform work at ZenteiQ spans Python and Go services, shared libraries, API gateways, authentication, event-driven integration, databases, CI/CD, and observability. I have implemented model-experiment controls and turn tracing, inference gateway components, Go data services, and reusable deployment tooling. I contribute to technical planning, code review, hiring, and coordination across research and engineering.

I joined ZenteiQ in June 2024. ZenteiQ builds physics-native scientific foundation models, pre-trained from scratch, which began at IISc Bangalore and are backed by the IndiaAI Mission. From June 2024 to March 2025, I was also a Project Associate at IISc’s AiREX Lab. Before ZenteiQ, I was a backend engineering intern at Contlo, where I moved campaign delivery from a Rails monolith to Kafka-based microservices. Earlier, I worked on Simulink Model Advisor reporting at MathWorks, explored sparse neural-network inference using GPUs at IIT Madras, and contributed to research on automated application processing at IIIT Bangalore. I graduated from IIIT Bangalore in 2024 with an Integrated M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering.

I want to contribute across the development of foundation models, from the data and objectives used in pre-training to adaptation, alignment, and evaluation. My current post-training and systems work gives me a practical perspective on how those choices interact.

For research and engineering conversations, you can email me at {{ site.data.main_info.email }} or find me on LinkedIn and GitHub.

Focus Areas

Research interests: pre-training, data, alignment, and efficient model training; how data, learning objectives, and scalable systems shape model capabilities and behavior.

Model training and alignment. My current work centers on supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, and preference-data workflows. My broader research interests include pre-training and the interaction between training stages, model capabilities, and behavior.

Data and evaluation. I contribute to synthetic-data and human-feedback workflows, safety-data design, red-teaming, multilingual evaluation, and release documentation. I focus on understanding failures and making evaluation evidence useful for model-development decisions.

ML systems and infrastructure. I work on systems supporting model experimentation and GPU/TPU inference, including inference gateways, generation controls, model and tool-call tracing, vLLM, Kubernetes, and deployment workflows. I am interested in scalable training systems and efficient experimentation.

Platforms and technical leadership. My platform work spans Python and Go services, API gateways, data workflows, databases, CI/CD, and developer tooling. I also contribute to technical planning, code review, hiring, and coordination across teams.
